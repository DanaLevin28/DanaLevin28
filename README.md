## 👋 Hi, I'm Dana

I'm a third-year Industrial Engineering & Management student at Ben-Gurion University, specializing in information systems. I like turning messy, manual processes into clear systems: I interview the people involved, map how the work really happens, model the data, and then build.

Before university I served as an officer in the Israeli Air Force, where I led teams. I also work as a fitness instructor, which is where my current project comes from: I run into the studio's real problems every week.

What I enjoy most is the link between people and technology. I like talking with people, understanding what they actually need, and turning that into something technical that fits them.

**Looking for:** student positions in data analysis, business analysis, PMO, product, and operations. Tel Aviv and central Israel.
